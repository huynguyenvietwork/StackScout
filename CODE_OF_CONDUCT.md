# Code of Conduct

StackScout is built by people from different countries, languages and levels of experience.
We want everyone who files an issue, opens a pull request or joins a discussion to feel welcome.

## Expected

- Be kind and assume good intent. Most people here are giving their free time.
- Critique ideas and code, never people. Explain the why, suggest a way forward.
- Write so non-native speakers can follow: short sentences, no sarcasm.
- Respect people's time: search existing issues first, keep threads on topic.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or personal attacks, in public or in private.
- Discrimination of any kind, including on gender, identity, origin, religion, age or ability.
- Publishing someone's private information without their consent.
- Spam, self-promotion unrelated to StackScout, or repeated off-topic posts.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies to all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, and any channel
that speaks for the project. Maintainers may edit or remove content, and warn, block or
ban anyone who breaks these rules, in proportion to what happened.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through the contact details on
their GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially.
